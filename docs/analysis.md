# what the numbers say
This is January 2026, deciding at 09:00 the day before. All 1,488 half-hours had both a forecast and an actual.

On average the forecast missed by 2,042 MW, which is 18.1% of the average wind (11,284 MW). It leaned high by 1,748 MW. Nine times out of ten it was no more than 4,140 MW above what actually blew. It leaned high on 27 of the 31 days, and the worst day was 28 Jan at +5,375 MW.

Distance matters. Forecasts published up to six hours before the half-hour missed by 1,664 MW on average, and the ones published 66 to 72 hours before missed by 2,432 MW.

How a buyer would use it: take the forecast and subtract the P90 margin. On 15 Jan the 09:00 forecast was 15,834 MW and the margin was 4,568 MW (from 12 earlier days), so plan on about 11,270 MW of wind and cover the rest from other supply.
